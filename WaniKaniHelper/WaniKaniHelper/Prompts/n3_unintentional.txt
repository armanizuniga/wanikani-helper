# Unintentional Actions (〜てしまう / 〜ちゃう)
〜てしまう expresses that something happened completely, often with regret or unintentional nuance: 食べてしまった (I ended up eating it / I ate it all).
Also used for completion without regret: 宿題(しゅくだい)をやってしまう (I'll get the homework done).
Casual contraction: 〜てしまう → 〜ちゃう; 〜でしまう → 〜じゃう: 食べちゃった、飲んじゃった.
Context and tone reveal whether the speaker regrets the action or simply completed it.
