# Easy and Difficult to Do (〜やすい、〜にくい、〜がたい、〜づらい)
〜やすい attaches to verb stem: "easy to do / tends to happen": 食べやすい (easy to eat), 壊(こわ)れやすい (breaks easily).
〜にくい: "hard to do / resistant": 読(よ)みにくい (hard to read), 開(あ)けにくい (hard to open).
〜がたい: formal, strong difficulty — often emotional or logical: 信(しん)じがたい (hard to believe).
〜づらい: difficulty with emotional weight or personal discomfort: 言(い)いづらい (hard to bring myself to say).
やすい/にくい describe physical or practical ease; づらい implies personal reluctance or discomfort.
