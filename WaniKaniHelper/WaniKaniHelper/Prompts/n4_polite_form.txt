# Polite Form (〜ます / です)
Attach ます to the verb stem for polite present/future: 食べます、書きます.
Negative: ません. Past: ました. Past-negative: ませんでした.
です follows nouns and adjectives in polite speech; plain だ is casual.
Use polite forms with strangers, coworkers, or in formal situations. Use plain forms with close friends and family.
Do not mix polite and plain forms in the same sentence — pick one register and stay consistent.
