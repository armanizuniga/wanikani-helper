# Potential Form (can/be able to)
Ru-verbs: replace る with られる → 食べられる (can eat). In casual speech often shortened to 食べれる.
U-verbs: change the final u sound to the e row + る → 書く→書ける, 飲む→飲める, 話す→話せる.
できる expresses general ability or possibility: 日本語(にほんご)ができる (I can speak Japanese).
見える (to be visible) and 聞こえる (to be audible) are naturally potential — do not conjugate them further.
The object of a potential verb may take が instead of を: ピアノが弾(ひ)ける.
