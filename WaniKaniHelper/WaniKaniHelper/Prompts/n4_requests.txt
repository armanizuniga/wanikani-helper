# Requests and Commands (てください、なさい、command form)
〜てください is the standard polite request: 座(すわ)ってください (please sit down).
〜ちょうだい is casual/feminine: ちょっと待(ま)ってちょうだい.
〜なさい is a firm parental/teacher instruction, not rude but not soft: 早(はや)く寝(ね)なさい.
Plain imperative (command form): u-verbs change u→e (書け), ru-verbs add ろ/よ (食べろ) — direct and blunt, used between close friends or in emergency.
Negative request 〜ないで: 触(さわ)らないで (don't touch).
