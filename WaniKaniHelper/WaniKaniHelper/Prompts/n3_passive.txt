# Passive (〜られる — be done to)
Ru-verbs: replace る with られる → 食べられる. U-verbs: change u→a + れる → 書かれる. する→される、くる→こられる.
Direct passive: the subject receives the action — ケーキが食(た)べられた (the cake was eaten).
Indirect/suffering passive: the subject is adversely affected — 雨(あめ)に降(ふ)られた (I was rained on / the rain came down on me).
Japanese passive is often used to show the subject as the affected party, not just to avoid naming the agent.
Passive is also used for politeness: こちらにご記入(きにゅう)いただけますか。
