# Negative Variations (〜ないで、〜ずに、ん negative、ぬ negative)
〜ないで: without doing / please don't: 急(いそ)がないで (without hurrying / don't hurry). Softer than するな.
〜ずに: formal equivalent of ないで — without doing: 何(なに)も言(い)わずに帰(かえ)った (left without saying anything).
ん negative: casual contraction of ない → 食べん、知らん (masculine, Kansai/casual).
ぬ negative: classical/literary — occasionally seen in set phrases: 知(し)らぬが仏(ほとけ) (ignorance is bliss).
ないで is the most common in everyday speech; ずに is preferred in formal writing and news.
