# Giving and Receiving (あげる、くれる、もらう)
あげる: speaker/in-group gives to someone else: 友達(ともだち)にプレゼントをあげた.
やる: give to a social inferior (animals, plants, children in some dialects) — use carefully.
くれる: someone gives to the speaker or in-group: 先生(せんせい)が本(ほん)をくれた.
もらう: speaker receives from someone — [giver] に/から + [receiver] が + もらう: 友達にもらった.
Favor expressions: てあげる (do for someone), てくれる (someone does for me), てもらう (have someone do for me).
The direction of giving/receiving determines which verb to use — always think from the speaker's perspective.
