# Question Marker か and Question Words
Add か to the end of a polite sentence to form a yes/no question: 行きますか。(Are you going?)
In casual speech, か can sound harsh — a rising intonation with の is softer: 行くの？
Question words: 何(なに/なん) what, どこ where, 誰(だれ) who, いつ when, どう how, なぜ/どうして why, どれ which, いくら how much.
Question words take が (not は) when they are the subject: 誰が来ますか。
Place the question word in the same position the answer would occupy — Japanese does not move it to the front.
