# Trying (てみる、ようとする)
〜てみる means to try something out and see what happens: 食(た)べてみる (try eating it), 使(つか)ってみる (try using it).
〜ようとする means to attempt or try hard to do: 起(お)きようとしたけど、起きられなかった (I tried to get up but couldn't).
てみる focuses on doing something as an experiment; ようとする emphasizes effort or intention.
Use てみる in everyday suggestions and casual speech — it's much more common than ようとする.
