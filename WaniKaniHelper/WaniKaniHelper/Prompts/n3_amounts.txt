# Amounts and Limits (だけ、しか〜ない、ばかり、すぎる、ほど)
だけ means "only / just that amount": 一(ひと)つだけ (just one).
しか〜ない means "nothing but / only" with a negative verb — stronger than だけ: 一つしかない (there's only one).
ばかり means "nothing but / just did": 甘(あま)いものばかり食べる (eats nothing but sweets) / 今(いま)来たばかり (just arrived).
すぎる attaches to verb stem or adjective stem to mean "too much/too many": 食べすぎ、高すぎる.
ほど indicates extent or degree: 死(し)ぬほど疲(つか)れた (tired to the point of dying / extremely tired).
